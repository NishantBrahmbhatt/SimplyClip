Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

SimplyClip does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Data we collect

We collect nothing. Specifically:

  • No name, email, or identifying information
  • No tracking of which videos you watch or download
  • No data sent to external servers
  • All video processing happens locally on your computer

How the extension works

The extension reads the current YouTube page URL and video metadata (title, duration, thumbnail) solely to display information in the popup. This data is never transmitted anywhere.

Downloads are processed by the SimplyClip Helper app running locally on your machine on port 9999. No data passes through any external server.

Permissions explained

activeTab — To read the current YouTube page URL and inject the Clip button.

storage — To save your quality preference and bracket timestamps between sessions.

downloads — To save the clipped video to your Downloads folder.

scripting — To inject the Clip button onto YouTube pages.

tabs — To identify which YouTube video is currently open.

offscreen — To process video data in the background.
