How it works

Three steps.
That's it.

01

Open any YouTube video

Navigate to any YouTube video. SimplyClip adds a ✂ Clip button next to the Subscribe button automatically.

02

Set your clip range

Drag the timeline handles or type exact timestamps. Choose your quality — 360p up to 1080p. Press [ and ] while watching to mark your clip points.

03

Download

Click Download Clip. Your MP4 saves directly to your Downloads folder. No ads, no redirects, no waiting.

Setup

One-time setup.
Works forever after.

SimplyClip needs a small helper app to process downloads. You install it once and never think about it again.

1

Add SimplyClip to Chrome

Visit the Chrome Web Store and click "Add to Chrome". The ✂ Clip button will appear on YouTube automatically.

2

Download the helper app

Click the SimplyClip icon in your Chrome toolbar, then click "Download Helper". Run the SimplyClip_Helper.exe file that downloads. Windows may show a security prompt — click "More info" then "Run anyway".

3

You're done

The helper starts automatically every time you boot your computer. Look for the SC icon in your system tray. Go to YouTube and start clipping.
